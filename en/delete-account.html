Delete Account

Last updated June 30, 2026

This page explains how to permanently delete your Bước Theo Chân Chúa account and all associated data. You have two options:

Fastest

Delete in the app

Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Data is removed immediately.

Request by email

When you can't access the app. Processed within 30 days.
1

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

Your profile (name, email, avatar)
All check-in history
Points earned and points history
Leaderboard entries
Mission progress
Streak records
Achievements unlocked

Anonymized aggregate statistics (e.g., total check-ins per church) may be retained without any link to your identity.

2

How to Delete Your Account In-App

1
Open the Bước Theo Chân Chúa app on your device.
2
Go to the Profile tab.
3
Tap Settings.
4
Select Delete Account.
5
Confirm the deletion when prompted.
